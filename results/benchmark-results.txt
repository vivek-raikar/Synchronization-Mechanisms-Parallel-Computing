# Synchronization Mechanisms Benchmark Results

**Workload:** 10,000,000 shared-counter increments  
**Thread counts:** 1, 2, 4, and 8  
**Environment:** Ubuntu running in VMware Workstation on the college PC  
**Timing unit:** Seconds

| Mechanism | Threads | Final Count | Correctness | Time (s) |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| None | 1 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.0069 |
| Critical | 1 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.1283 |
| Atomic | 1 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.0693 |
| Lock | 1 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.1163 |
| None | 2 | 5,220,657 | Incorrect | 0.0133 |
| Critical | 2 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.2595 |
| Atomic | 2 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.1484 |

| None | 4 | 7,279,449 | Incorrect | 0.0086 |

| Atomic | 4 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.1624 |

| None | 8 | 2,469,491 | Incorrect | 0.0103 |

| Atomic | 8 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.1757 |
| Lock | 8 | 10,000,000 | Correct | 0.4856 |

## Observations

- **None:** No synchronization overhead, but shared updates were lost in multithreaded runs.
- **Critical:** Correct results, with increasing execution time as contention grows.
- **Atomic:** Correct results and lower execution time than Critical and Lock in these measurements.
- **Lock:** Correct results, but comparatively high synchronization overhead.

These timings are measurements from one environment and may vary on other machines. The barrier and semaphore programs are separate demonstrations and are not included in this four-mechanism timing table.
